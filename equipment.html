Brewing Guide

Brewing Equipment Usage Tips

Simple tips to help you get the best results from your equipment.

French Press

Use coarse-ground coffee and hot water. Let the coffee steep for around four minutes before slowly pressing the filter down.

Pour-Over Dripper

Pour hot water slowly over the coffee grounds in a circular motion to help achieve an even extraction.

Coffee Tamper

Distribute the coffee grounds evenly in the portafilter, then press down firmly and levelly before brewing.

Hand Grinder

Choose the appropriate grind size for your brewing method and grind the beans shortly before making your coffee.

Espresso Machine

Use finely ground coffee and distribute it evenly in the portafilter. Tamp the grounds firmly and evenly before starting the extraction.

Cold Brew Maker

Add coarse-ground coffee and cold water to the brewer. Leave it to steep before removing the filter and serving the cold brew.
